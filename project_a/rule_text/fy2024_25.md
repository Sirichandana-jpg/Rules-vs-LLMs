# Income-tax rules for a salaried resident individual, FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)
(Summary written by the author from the Income-tax Act and Finance Acts 2024; clause tags in [brackets] must be cited in any trace.
 Verify every number against incometax.gov.in before relying on it. Scope: salary income only.)

## Common
[Sec 15-17 salary] Start from annual gross salary (includes HRA received).
[Sec 16(ia)] Standard deduction from salary: Old regime Rs 50,000; New regime Rs 75,000 (both capped at salary).
[Sec 14] Gross total income (GTI) = salary after the deductions below (old regime) or salary - standard deduction (new regime).
[Sec 288A] Taxable income is rounded: ignore any fraction of a rupee, then round to the nearest Rs 10 (5 rounds up).
[Sec 288B] Final tax payable is rounded the same way (fraction ignored, nearest Rs 10, 5 rounds up).
[Finance Act cess 4%] Health and education cess = 4% of (tax + surcharge).

## Old regime (deductions allowed)
[Sec 10(13A); Rule 2A] HRA exemption = max(0, least of: (a) HRA received, (b) rent paid - 10% of basic+DA, (c) 50% of basic+DA if the city is Delhi/Mumbai/Kolkata/Chennai else 40%). Zero if no rent or no HRA.
[Sec 24(b)] Interest on loan for a self-occupied house: loss from house property up to Rs 2,00,000 set off against salary.
[Sec 80C] Max Rs 1,50,000.   [Sec 80CCD(1B)] Additional NPS, max Rs 50,000.
[Sec 80D] Health insurance premium: self/spouse/children max Rs 25,000 (Rs 50,000 if the taxpayer is 60+); parents max Rs 25,000 (Rs 50,000 if parents are 60+). 
[Sec 80A(2) overall cap] Chapter VI-A deductions cannot exceed gross total income.
[Finance Act First Schedule Pt I (slab rates)] Old-regime slabs on taxable income:
 - age below 60: 0-2,50,000 nil; 2,50,001-5,00,000 5%; 5,00,001-10,00,000 20%; above 10,00,000 30%.
 - age 60 to 79: nil up to 3,00,000; then 5% to 5,00,000; 20% to 10,00,000; 30% above.
 - age 80+: nil up to 5,00,000; 20% to 10,00,000; 30% above.
[Sec 87A] Rebate (old): if taxable income <= Rs 5,00,000, rebate = min(tax, Rs 12,500). No marginal relief.

## New regime (Sec 115BAC(1A) default regime; no HRA, 80C, 80D, home-loan deductions; age does not matter)
[Sec 115BAC(1A)] Slabs: 0-3,00,000 nil; 3,00,001-7,00,000 5%; 7,00,001-10,00,000 10%; 10,00,001-12,00,000 15%; 12,00,001-15,00,000 20%; above 15,00,000 30%.
[Sec 87A] Rebate (new): if taxable income <= Rs 7,00,000, rebate = min(tax, Rs 25,000). Marginal relief: if income is above 7,00,000, tax (after rebate) cannot exceed the income above 7,00,000.

## Surcharge (on tax after rebate; salary-only income)
[Finance Act First Schedule Pt I (surcharge)] Old: income above 50 lakh 10%; above 1 crore 15%; above 2 crore 25%; above 5 crore 37%. New: same but capped at 25%.
Marginal relief: tax + surcharge cannot exceed (tax payable on the threshold income incl. its own surcharge rate) + (income above the threshold).
